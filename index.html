Who We Are

Comprehensive FM for Public Sector & Community

CHFM Group is a full-service facilities management company with over three decades of experience working alongside local authorities, housing associations, and commercial clients. One of the UK’s most accredited FM providers — recognised for safety, quality, sustainability, and genuine community impact.

From planned preventative maintenance and reactive repairs to energy management and full regulatory compliance, we deliver integrated solutions that keep buildings safe, efficient, and fit for purpose.

Public-Focused FM, Built Around You

Specialist FM for public sector and community-focused organisations.

Each sector we serve has its own regulatory demands and operational challenges. CHFM Group has built specialist teams and service methodologies for each.

Local Authorities

Housing Associations

Charities, faith organisations, and voluntary sector groups operate critical community facilities with limited budgets. We provide affordable, flexible maintenance services that keep these vital buildings safe and compliant.
